# Troubleshoot samples

Banked game-region screenshots from live play sessions. In the project these shots are troubleshooting evidence: each one pairs a game pane (read by eye — never OCR output as ground truth) with the capture log from the same moment, so missed lines and misreads can be investigated offline.

- `zelda_*_game.png` — The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (Japanese), dialogue, signs, cutscenes, menus.
- `ffvi_*_game.png` — Final Fantasy VI (Japanese), dialogue, battle UI, menus.
- `papermario_*_game.png` — Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Japanese), dialogue and UI.

10 Zelda, 10 FFVI, 7 Paper Mario (all that existed at export) — 27 total. Full sessions bank hundreds; these show the process, not the dataset.
